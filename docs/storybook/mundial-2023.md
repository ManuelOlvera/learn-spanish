# Las campeonas 🥇

*The champions* — story id `mundial-2023`

> ✅ **Illustrated.** These are the prompts that produced the art currently
> shipping. Kept so a page can be regenerated in the same style.

A true story: the 2023 Women's World Cup final in Sydney, Spain 1–0 England, Olga Carmona scoring in the 29th minute.

Eight ready-to-paste prompts. Each block is **complete**: copy everything
between the fences, paste it into ChatGPT / the Codex app / Gemini, and save
the result. No editing, no assembly.

Two things that materially improve what comes back:

- **Generate all eight in one conversation**, in order, so the model can see
  the pictures it already made and keep the characters identical. From prompt 2
  onward you can add *"same characters and style as the previous image"*.
- **If the tool has an aspect-ratio control, set it to 4:3 there too.** The
  prompt says so in words, but a UI setting usually beats prompt text.

**Save as** `apps/web/art-source/mundial-2023/1.png` … `8.png`, then run `pnpm art`
from the repo root. Full workflow: [`../../apps/web/src/story-art/README.md`](../../apps/web/src/story-art/README.md).

## 1 — `mundial-2023-1.png`

*“En 2023, la selección española de mujeres llegó a su primera final del Mundial.” — Spain's women reached their first final.*

```text
Children's picture-book illustration for a 4-year-old. Flat vector style: bold dark-brown outlines of even weight, like a thick marker. Flat solid colour fills — no gradients, no texture, no shading, no 3D. Simple rounded shapes, cheerful and calm. Wide 4:3 landscape composition. The illustration must fill the entire frame edge to edge — no white border, no mat, no frame, no rounded corners around the picture. Keep important elements away from the very edges so nothing is clipped.

CAMERA — this matters more than anything else here: draw every scene from **pitch level**, as if standing on the touchline a few metres from the action. NEVER an aerial, bird's-eye or whole-stadium view. The players are the subject and must be large in the frame; the crowd is only a band of colour in the background behind them. Never draw the stadium bowl seen from outside or above, and never place a large figure on top of a distant stadium — that breaks the scale.

THE SPANISH PLAYERS: generic cartoon women footballers in plain red shirts and dark blue shorts, hair tied back, no faces of real people, no names or numbers on the shirts. Friendly rounded figures. The same look in every picture.
THE OPPONENTS: generic cartoon women footballers in plain white shirts, no faces of real people, no names or numbers.
THE TROPHY: it must be recognisably the real World Cup trophy, NOT a generic cup with two handles. Solid gold, and shaped like two stylised human figures standing back to back whose bodies and arms spiral upward and outward to hold a round globe above their heads. The whole form twists as it rises from a small round dark-green base. No handles. Same trophy in every picture that shows one.

IMPORTANT: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, words, or signs anywhere in the image.

The scene: A team of women footballers in red shirts standing together in a line on a green pitch, arms around each other, proud and hopeful.
```
